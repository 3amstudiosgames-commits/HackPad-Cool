# HackPad-Cool
A 9 switch, 9 LED, 1 rotary switch macropad powered by MOUDLE-SEEEDUINO-XIAO made for a YSWS on stardance mission hackpad.
## Features:
3d printed case, 9 keys, 9 LED, 1 rotary encoder, 1 Seeed XIAO RP2040 + coded keys
## BOM/ bill of materials:
- 1 unsoldered Seeed XIAO RP2040
- 9x through-hole 1N4148 Diodes
- 9x MX-Style switches
- 1x EC11E Rotary encoders (with switch!)
- 9x white blank DSA keycaps
- 16x SK6812 MINI-E LEDs
- 6x M3x16mm screws
- 6x M3x5mx4mm heatset inserts
- Soldering iron
- 3D printing case
- pcb
